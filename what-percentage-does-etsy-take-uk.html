What percentage does Etsy take in the UK?

About 13.2% of the order total, plus about 42p per order, if you sell from the UK with a UK bank account and haven't given Etsy a VAT ID. With a VAT ID it's about 11%, plus about 35p. The order total includes what the buyer pays you for postage, so Etsy takes its share of that too.

The listing fee is 0.20 USD and Etsy converts it at the market rate when it's charged. At the European Central Bank reference rate for 16 Nov 2026 (1 USD = £0.75767) that's about 15p, so the pence move a little with the exchange rate. Etsy charges it when you list or auto-renew, not out of the sale itself, but every item you sell has cost one, so we count it per order. The 20% is the UK standard VAT rate from GOV.UK; Etsy's VAT page doesn't state a rate.

Worked example: a £20 order

A buyer pays £16 for the item and £4 for postage, so the order total is £20. No VAT ID, and the sale didn't come from an Offsite Ad.

Transaction fee (6.5% of £20)£1.30
Payment processing (4% of £20 + £0.20)£1.00
Regulatory Operating fee (0.48% of £20)£0.10
Listing / auto-renew fee (0.20 USD)£0.15
Fees before VAT£2.55
VAT on those fees (20%)£0.51
Etsy takes£3.06

That's 15.3% of the £20, more than the 13.2% headline because of the fixed pence. With a VAT ID it would be £2.55, about 12.7%. On cheap items the fixed part matters more: on a £5 order the same fees come to about 21.6% without a VAT ID.

When Etsy takes more

  • Offsite Ads: 15% of the order total, only when Etsy says the order came from one of its ads on another site. It's 12% if your shop has ever made 10,000 USD or more in any 365-day period, and never more than 100 USD per order. On the £20 order above that's another £3 before VAT, or £3.60 if you have no VAT ID. Shops that have always made less than 10,000 USD in any 365-day period can opt out of Offsite Ads; above that, they're compulsory.
  • Each extra item bought from a multi-quantity listing costs another 0.20 USD listing fee.
  • Etsy Ads, postage labels and the one-off shop set-up fee are on top, and depend on what you choose.
