The price you guessed is missing two costs.

Your time

Pouring, curing, packing and listing are work. If your time isn't in the cost, you're paying for the business with it.

The fees

Transaction, processing and listing fees come out of every sale. A price that covers your supplies can still leave almost nothing.

Batch maths

You buy wax by the pound and fragrance by the ounce, then pour a dozen at once. Splitting that per candle by hand is where mistakes creep in.

How it works

1

Add your supplies

Enter what you paid and how much you got. Tallymade works out the cost per gram, ounce, millilitre or piece.

2

Build the batch

Add each ingredient by amount or as a percentage of another — like an 8% fragrance load — plus your hands-on minutes.

3

Pick where you sell

Etsy (US/UK), Square card readers and cash are built in, each with its source and date. Get the price that keeps your profit target.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your materials and products are stored only on your phone. There's no sign-up.

What if I change phones?

Export a backup file from Settings and restore it on the new phone. Tallymade doesn't keep a copy on a server.

Which fees are included?

Presets for Etsy (US and UK sellers), Square card readers (US and UK) and cash. Each shows its source and the date it was checked. Every fee line is editable, and you can add your own channels.

Why isn't TikTok Shop built in?

Its published rates vary by category and region, so we couldn't confirm one set of numbers. Add it as your own channel using the rates in your seller account.

Which currencies work?

USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD. Pick the currency you sell in; Tallymade doesn't convert between currencies.

Can I mix weight and volume?

Yes — add a density (grams per ml from your supplier's data sheet) to a material.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed by Apple or Google. Cancel in your store account's subscription settings; refunds are requested through the store.

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. Tallymade is a pricing calculator. For tax and bookkeeping, talk to an accountant.
