Etsy fees for UK sellers, line by line

If your Etsy payment account is in pounds and your bank is in the UK, a sale can carry up to five charges: the transaction fee, the payment processing fee, the Regulatory Operating fee, an Offsite Ads fee if the order came from one of Etsy's ads, and VAT on Etsy's fees unless you have given Etsy a VAT ID. The listing fee comes on top. Here is each one, where Etsy states it, and what they add up to on a real-sized order.

The fees on every sale

  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the total order amount, including what you charge for postage and gift wrap. Source: Etsy Help, What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy? and Etsy Fee Basics. Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Payment processing: 4% + £0.20 per order, for a bank account in the United Kingdom. It is taken from the total sale price, including postage. Source: Etsy Help, What are Payment Processing Fees for Selling on Etsy? (table row "United Kingdom: 4% + 0.20 GBP"). Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Regulatory Operating fee: 0.48% for UK sellers, charged on the item price plus postage and gift wrap. Source: Etsy Help, What is a Regulatory Operating Fee? Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • VAT on those fees: 20%, if you haven't added a VAT ID to your Etsy account. 20% is the UK standard VAT rate from GOV.UK; Etsy's pages don't state a rate. Etsy says it may collect VAT on seller fees and on Etsy Payments processing fees, and that the Regulatory Operating fee is subject to VAT where applicable. If you have added a VAT ID, you aren't charged and get a monthly invoice saying so. Etsy's pages don't spell out the VAT treatment of the listing fee or the Offsite Ads fee, so the example below puts VAT only on the three sale fees above. Sources: Etsy Help, How VAT Is Collected on Seller Fees, the processing and Regulatory Operating fee pages above; GOV.UK, VAT rates (standard rate 20%). Checked 11 Nov 2026.

Fees that don't come with every sale

  • Listing fee: 0.20 USD per listing, paid whether or not it sells. A listing lasts 4 months. You pay it again when a listing auto-renews, when an auto-renew listing sells, and for each extra item bought from a multi-quantity listing. Etsy publishes fixed fees in USD and converts them at the market rate when they hit your payment account, so the pound amount moves with the exchange rate. Source: Etsy Help, What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy? Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Offsite Ads: 15% of the order total, only on orders Etsy attributes to one of its ads on another site (a buyer clicks the ad and buys from your shop within 30 days). It drops to 12% once your shop has made 10,000 USD or more in any 365-day period, and never exceeds 100 USD per order. Below that 10,000 USD level you can opt out in Shop Manager → Settings → Offsite Ads; above it, taking part is required. Source: Etsy Help, How Etsy's Offsite Ads Work. Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Currency conversion: 2.5%, only if you list in a different currency from your bank account (for example, listing in USD with a UK bank account). Source: Etsy Help, What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy? Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Shop set-up fee: a one-time, non-refundable fee when you open a shop. Etsy says the cost varies by location, and the amount is shown to you during shop set-up, in the "Total due now" section, before you pay. Source: Etsy Help, How to Open an Etsy Shop and What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy? Checked 11 Nov 2026.
  • Etsy Ads and postage labels cost whatever you choose to spend or buy.

Worked example: a £23.50 order

A buyer orders one item at £20 and pays £3.50 postage, so the order total is £23.50. You have no VAT ID, and the sale didn't come from an Offsite Ad.

Transaction fee (6.5%)£1.53
Payment processing (4% + £0.20)£1.14
Regulatory Operating fee (0.48%)£0.11
Fees before VAT£2.78
VAT on those fees (20%)£0.56
Etsy fees on this order£3.34

That is about 14.2% of the order total, before the listing fee (0.20 USD). With a VAT ID it would be £2.78, about 11.8%. Notice that the fees are charged on the postage too: if your postage only covers the stamp, Etsy's share of it comes out of your profit.

If the same order had come from an Offsite Ad, the 15% ad fee would add £3.53 (excluding VAT).

The example also leaves out any sales tax or VAT added to the buyer's order, and personalisation costs. Etsy says processing fees are taken from the total sale price including any applicable sales tax, and that the Regulatory Operating fee is charged on gift wrap and personalisation costs where they apply, so either would raise the fees a little.

One item per order, free postage (postage built into the price), no Offsite Ads, listing fee left out. The fixed 20p processing charge is why fees bite harder on cheap items.
